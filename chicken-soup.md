# Chicken Soup

A comforting, classic chicken soup made from scratch. Perfect for cold days or when you need a little extra warmth.

- **Prep time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook time:** 1 hour 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken (about 1.5 kg), or 4 chicken thighs/breasts
- 2 liters water (or chicken stock for extra flavor)
- 2 carrots, peeled and sliced
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup egg noodles (optional)
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the aromatics.** Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 5–7 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
2. **Add the chicken and liquid.** Place the chicken in the pot and pour in the water or stock. Add the bay leaves and thyme.
3. **Simmer.** Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for about 1 hour, until the chicken is fully cooked and tender. Skim off any foam from the surface.
4. **Shred the chicken.** Remove the chicken from the pot. Let it cool slightly, then discard the skin and bones and shred the meat. Return the meat to the pot.
5. **Cook the noodles (optional).** Add the egg noodles and simmer for 8–10 minutes, until tender.
6. **Season and serve.** Remove the bay leaves. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley.

## Tips

- For a richer broth, use bone-in chicken and simmer longer.
- Add a squeeze of lemon juice at the end for brightness.
- Store leftovers in the fridge for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
